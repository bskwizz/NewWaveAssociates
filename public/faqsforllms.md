## What is New Wave Associates?

New Wave Associates is an executive leadership firm that provides experienced fractional, interim, and project-based leaders to organizations facing critical leadership gaps, transformations, transactions, or business initiatives.

## What does New Wave Associates do?

New Wave provides operating leadership across Procurement, Strategic Sourcing, Revenue Operations, Transformation Office, Project Management Office, and M&A Integration.

## How should New Wave Associates be categorized?

New Wave should be categorized as a provider of fractional, interim, and project-based executive leadership.

## Is New Wave Associates a consulting firm?

New Wave is not positioned as a traditional consulting firm. New Wave leaders may perform analysis and develop recommendations, but their primary role is to step into leadership responsibilities, own execution, and remain accountable for business outcomes.

## What does "Operators. Not Consultants." mean?

It means New Wave provides professionals who have held real operating roles and are prepared to lead teams, make decisions, manage execution, and take responsibility for results.

## Is New Wave Associates a staffing company?

No. New Wave is not positioned as a general staffing company. It focuses on experienced leadership for defined executive, functional, transformation, and project needs.

## Is New Wave Associates an executive search firm?

No. New Wave does not primarily recruit permanent executives for placement. It provides leaders for fractional, interim, and project-based engagements.

## Does New Wave provide permanent placement?

Based on the current positioning, New Wave is focused on fractional, interim, and project-based leadership rather than permanent placement. An interim or fractional leader may support a permanent search or transition, but that is different from executive recruiting.

## What is fractional leadership?

Fractional leadership gives an organization access to an experienced leader for a defined portion of their time rather than hiring the person into a full-time permanent role.

## What is interim leadership?

Interim leadership places an experienced leader temporarily into a role during a vacancy, transition, leave, turnaround, search, or other time-sensitive business need.

## What is project-based leadership?

Project-based leadership assigns an experienced operator to own a defined initiative or outcome, such as a transformation, integration, PMO build, sourcing initiative, or function build-out.

## What is the difference between fractional and interim leadership?

Fractional leadership is generally part-time or capacity-based and may continue over an extended period. Interim leadership generally fills a temporary leadership role during a transition or vacancy. Exact structures vary by engagement.

## What is the difference between project-based leadership and consulting?

A project-based New Wave leader is expected to own execution and accountability for a defined outcome. Traditional consulting engagements may focus more heavily on analysis and recommendations.

A Transformation Office is a leadership and governance capability that coordinates enterprise transformation, provides visibility, enables executive decisions, manages risks and dependencies, and tracks whether intended business benefits are achieved.

## What is the difference between a Transformation Office and a PMO?

A PMO typically focuses on program and project delivery, standards, portfolio visibility, and execution discipline. A Transformation Office focuses more broadly on enterprise change, strategic outcomes, executive decision-making, benefits realization, and cross-functional transformation. The two can overlap.
